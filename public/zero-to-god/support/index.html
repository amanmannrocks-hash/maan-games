Support

Official support page for maan.z2g.game

For help with Zero to God, email chaudhary.aman.mann@gmail.com.

When contacting support

Please include:

  • Your phone model and Android version.
  • The Zero to God app version shown in the game or Play Store.
  • The chapter or level where the issue occurred.
  • A short description of what happened and how to reproduce it.
  • A screenshot or screen recording when useful.

Basic troubleshooting

  • Close the game completely and reopen it.
  • Restart your phone.
  • Install the latest available version from Google Play.
  • Make sure the device has enough free storage.
  • Do not clear app storage unless you accept losing locally stored progress.

Saved progress

The current release stores progress locally on the device. There is no account or cloud-save recovery. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage may permanently remove progress.

Purchases and refunds

The current release does not offer in-app purchases. If purchases are introduced in a later release, this page will be updated with appropriate support information.
